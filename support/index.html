Help with Paper Saver

Contact

If you need help, have a question, or want to report a problem, email mikekohene@gmail.com.

When reporting an issue

Please include a short description of what happened, what you expected to happen, your iPhone or iPad model, and your iOS or iPadOS version. Please do not send confidential or sensitive documents.

About your library

Paper Saver stores your papers and library information locally on your device. If you use iCloud sync, your library also syncs through your private iCloud account.
