Help with PaperOrganizer

Contact

If you need help, have a question, or want to report a problem, email your-email@example.com.

When reporting an issue

Please include a short description of what happened, what you expected to happen, your iPhone or iPad model, and your iOS or iPadOS version. Please do not send confidential or sensitive documents.

About your library

PaperOrganizer stores your papers and library information locally on your device. If you use iCloud sync, your library also syncs through your private iCloud account.
