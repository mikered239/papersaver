PaperOrganizer GitHub Pages site — repository: papersaver

Upload the CONTENTS of this folder directly to the root of your "papersaver" repository.

Your repository should look like:
  index.html
  styles.css
  assets/
  support/
  privacy/

GitHub Pages setup:
  Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > /(root)

Your URLs will be:
  Main:    https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/papersaver/
  Support: https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/papersaver/support/
  Privacy: https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/papersaver/privacy/

Before publishing, replace every occurrence of:
  your-email@example.com
with your real support email address.
